# 8D Checklist: a 21-item check before you send an 8D report

A plain-Markdown checklist covering D1 to D8 of an 8D problem-solving report. It gives D4 its own occurrence and escape focus: an occurrence root cause **and** an escape (non-detection) root cause. Copy it into your own form, a ticket or a wiki page.

**[checklist.md](checklist.md)**: 21 items in 5 sections (D1-D3, D4, D5-D6, D7-D8, final check).

## Use with care
Advisory only. It is a generic memory aid, not a standard and not a substitute for your customer's requirements. Your customer's 8D form, supplier manual and customer-specific requirements come first. Ticking every box does not mean a customer will accept a report. It may help you spot gaps a reviewer could ask about.
