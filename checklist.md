# 8D / D4 Root-Cause Checklist (8D Gate)

Use before you send an 8D to your customer. A "no" is a gap a customer may question.

## D1-D3: Team, problem, containment
- [ ] Team named with roles (incl. someone from the process where it escaped).
- [ ] D2 is quantified: what, where, when, how many, how detected (is / is-not).
- [ ] Customer part numbers, lot/date codes and the defect photo are referenced.
- [ ] Containment covers ALL stock: plant, in transit, warehouse, customer site.
- [ ] Containment results are numbers (parts sorted / found NOK), not "done".
- [ ] Containment does not hide the problem (sorting is not a root cause fix).

## D4: Root cause
- [ ] Two root causes stated: **occurrence** (why it was made) AND **escape / non-detection** (why it was not caught).
- [ ] The escape point is named: the first process step that should have caught it.
- [ ] 5-Why or Ishikawa reaches a controllable process cause, not "operator error" or "human mistake".
- [ ] Every cause candidate is shown as verified or ruled out, with evidence (test, data, reproduction).
- [ ] The defect was reproduced by switching the root cause ON and OFF (or equivalent proof).
- [ ] Systemic cause considered: why did the PFMEA / control plan allow it?

## D5-D6: Actions
- [ ] One permanent action per root cause (occurrence AND escape).
- [ ] Actions are specific (who, what, by when), not "retrain operators".
- [ ] Effectiveness verified with data after implementation (criterion set in advance, sized to the defect rate: https://8d-gate.vercel.app/capa-effectiveness-check?utm_source=github&utm_medium=repo&utm_campaign=8d-checklist).
- [ ] Containment removed only after verification.

## D7-D8: Prevent recurrence
- [ ] PFMEA, control plan, work instructions updated (with revision numbers).
- [ ] Lessons learned applied to similar parts / lines.
- [ ] Customer-specific requirements and deadlines (from the customer's supplier manual or SCAR) are met.

## Final check
- [ ] A reader outside your plant can follow it without asking a question.
- [ ] Every claim has evidence attached or referenced.
